### Domain, web & email
- [ ] Register a domain at Cloudflare Registrar (sold at cost, roughly $10/year for a .com) and use Cloudflare's free DNS
- [ ] Set up free company email: Cloudflare Email Routing to receive at hello@ and billing@, forwarded to your existing Gmail
- [ ] Set up Gmail "Send mail as" so you can reply from your company address, then add SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records
- [ ] Build the website as a static site (Astro or Hugo) and host it free on Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages. Avoid Vercel Hobby, which is restricted to non-commercial use
- [ ] Fill in the `[your email or website]` placeholder in `profile/README.md` with the real contact info
- [ ] Reserve social handles (LinkedIn company page, X, etc.) that match the brand

### Customer data & ops
- [ ] Start the customer tracker in Google Sheets or Baserow (free), with fields for contact, company, status, deal value, contract dates, notes
- [ ] Move to an open-source CRM (Twenty or EspoCRM) or a Supabase free-tier table only once the sheet gets unwieldy

- [ ] Turn on MFA for every account, and keep the recovery codes somewhere safe
- [ ] Set up free scheduling with Cal.com or a Google Calendar booking page
